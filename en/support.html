Support — VizoaCast

For a failed stream, check the provider address, credentials, provider subscription and simultaneous connection limit. Prefer HTTPS. The player cannot supply or repair a provider's content. Do not send passwords, complete source URLs containing credentials, Apple purchase proofs or private transaction identifiers in support messages.

For purchase problems, use Restore Purchases and check Apple account subscriptions. Playback requires an active Apple subscription. Your IPTV provider determines source connection limits. iCloud is optional for library synchronization. Deleting a source does not cancel billing.
