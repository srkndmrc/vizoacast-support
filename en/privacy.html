Privacy — VizoaCast 1.0.0 · 2026-11-02

VizoaCast plays media from sources you supply. There are no advertising or tracking SDKs. Provider passwords are stored in Keychain; source addresses, library favorites, watched state and playback progress are stored on your device. When you choose iCloud synchronization, source credentials may synchronize through iCloud Keychain and library/source records through your private CloudKit database. Apple operates these services. Reminders stay on this device.

Apple handles purchases and renewals. VizoaCast checks subscription access on the device through StoreKit. Your provider receives media requests directly.

Playback requires an active Apple subscription. Browsing the local library requires no subscription. Removing a source deletes local source records and credentials in the app. Manage subscription billing in your Apple account settings.
